WorkTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

WorkTrack is a practice management web app for chartered accountancy firms. This page explains what information the app handles, including when a firm connects its Google Drive.

Information we handle

  • Account details: name, email address and password (stored as a secure hash by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Firm details: firm name and address, working hours and days, office location and geofence, reporting hierarchy, and employee names, designations and access levels.
  • Work records: tasks, timesheets, invoices and similar records that the firm creates in the app.

Google Drive access

If the person who registered the firm (normally the Managing Partner) chooses to connect Google Drive, WorkTrack requests only the drive.file permission. With this permission the app can see and change only the folders and files that WorkTrack itself creates. It cannot open or list any other file in that Google account.

WorkTrack uses this access to create a firm folder and store the firm's documents, records and backups in it. Other employees do not need to sign in with Google or share their own Drive.

The authorisation token that lets WorkTrack reach the folder is encrypted and kept on our server. It is never sent to a web browser.

How we use Google data

Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the features the firm has asked for, namely storing and retrieving its own files in its own Drive folder. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as needed to run the service. No person reads this data except as required for security, to comply with law, or with the firm's consent. WorkTrack's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where information is stored

Account and firm registry information is stored in a Supabase database. Documents, backups and records that a firm saves to Drive are stored in that firm's own Google Drive.

Disconnecting and deleting

The Managing Partner can disconnect Google Drive at any time from Settings, which revokes WorkTrack's access with Google. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in Drive stay in that account until the owner deletes them. To have account or firm records removed from our database, contact us at the address below.

Security

Access to firm data is limited to signed-in members of that firm, and sensitive settings can be changed only by the Managing Partner. No system is perfectly secure, but we use encryption in transit and server-side controls to protect information.
